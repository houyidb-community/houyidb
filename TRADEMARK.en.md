You may use the Marks:

- to redistribute unmodified official binaries with their original notices.

If you distribute a modified version of HouyiDB, you **must not** use the
HouyiDB name or logo in a way that suggests it is the official project or is
endorsed by us. Rename your distribution and remove the logo. A factual
statement such as "derived from HouyiDB" is acceptable.

You may not use the Marks in product names, company names, domain names, or

their commercial agreement.

Use of the Marks does not imply any sponsorship, certification, or endorsement
by the HouyiDB project.

## 6. Reporting misuse

Report misleading use of the Marks through the repository's private security
advisory channel (see `SECURITY.en.md`). We prefer resolving issues informally
before taking formal action.
